I'm Aldrin John Dapetilla, a web designer and WordPress developer based in the Philippines. For more than four years I've designed and built websites for US businesses, mostly roofers, landscapers, home service companies and churches.

I enjoy the part of web design where good layout meets real results: a phone number that's easy to tap, a page that ranks, a section the client can update on their own. Lately I've added AI automation to my skills, so the systems behind a website can work as well as the site itself.

I'm looking for agencies that want a dependable designer and developer who cares about doing the work right.
